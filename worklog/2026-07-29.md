## BE — Align OpenAPI for assessments routes (dev)

**Task:** Fix the OpenAPI mismatch on `/v1/assessments*` in
`contracts/openapi.yaml`. The contract advertised workspace-scoped paths as
`/v1/assessments` + `X-Workspace-Id` header, but every implemented handler in
`src/modules/assessments/adapters/http/` is path-scoped under
`/v1/workspaces/:workspaceId/assessments`. Frontend codegen against the old
artifact would never resolve.

**Patch (contract-only):**
- `contracts/openapi.yaml`

    `workspaceId` as required path param.
  - `/v1/assessments/{assessmentId}` (GET) → same shape under
    `/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/assessments/{assessmentId}`.
  - Removed the legacy `/v1/assessments/{assessmentId}/finalize` and
    `/v1/assessments/{assessmentId}/exports` paths (no live handler for either;
    finalize lives at
    `/v1/workspaces/:workspaceId/assessments/:assessmentId/versions/:versionId/finalize`
    per `questionReviewRoutes.ts`, exports under
    `/v1/assessments/:id/output` per `artifactRoutes.ts` — out of scope here).
  - Blueprint paths re-anchored on a real `workspaceId` path param instead of
    the `WorkspaceId` header.
  - `CreateAssessmentRequest` schema aligned with the body actually consumed
    by `routes.ts` (`title`, `curriculumVersionId`, `blueprintItems[]` with
    `sequence / questionType / difficulty`).
